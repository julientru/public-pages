The Shipyard

Every choice trades something away. Weight slows you down, speed costs you armor, and a bigger crew takes a bigger share.

1

Each round has 3 steps. Give each of your ships up to 3 orders, in the order you want them carried out. In every step whoever started the fight acts first: you, when you raid or engage; the navy, when a patrol runs you down. Merchants always act last. The red tag above each enemy shows what it will do in each step.

Round shot smashes hulls. Chain shot shreds sails so they can't flee. Grapeshot and muskets kill crew at medium or close range. Cannons must be reloaded after each shot.

Close in to close range, then Board to capture a ship and all its gold. Sinking a merchant loses most of its gold. Beaten merchants may surrender to a black flag.
