🎈 Kids Games 🎈

Pick a game and have fun!

🚧 Games for this many players are coming soon!

🧱 Blocks Turn and drop the falling blocks to fill whole lines. 🐸 Jumpy Frog Jump over the rocks. How far can you go? 🃏 Memory Match Flip the cards and find all the pairs. 💡 Lights Out Tap to flip the lights. Can you turn them all off? 🧩 Slide Puzzle Slide the numbers until they are in order. 🍎 Picture Sudoku Every fruit once in each row, column and box. 🎵 Copy Me Watch the animals light up, then copy them. 🗼 Tower Move Move the tower, one ring at a time. 🔁 What's Next? Find the pattern and pick what comes next. 🐭 Mouse Maze Help the mouse find the cheese. 🔍 Odd One Out Which picture does not belong? 🌊 Color Flood Fill the board with one color before you run out of moves. ❌ Tic-Tac-Toe Three in a row against the robot. 🪖 Army Rush Stop the silly army in 2D, 3D or FPS! Shoot boxes for better weapons and more soldiers. ⛴️ Hong Kong Ferries Send the right ferry to the pier so everyone gets home to their island! ⚡ Snap Match Face to face: who finds the same picture first? 🍎 Apple Grab Face to face: take 1, 2 or 3 apples. Grab the last one to win! 🔴🟡 Four in a Row Take turns dropping discs. Line up four!
