🪖 Army Rush

🌊 1/12 ❤️ 100 🪨 Stones

Your army shoots by itself. Slide your finger on the field, hold ⬅️ ➡️, or use the arrow keys to aim. Shoot a box to open it: a new weapon (your army starts again with one of it), more of your weapon, a bigger wall or a shield. You can only pick one box of each pair, and a weapon you miss never comes back! Grey walls just block your shots. The enemy breaks your wall first, then hits your ❤️.
